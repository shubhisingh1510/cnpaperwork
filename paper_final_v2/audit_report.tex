\section*{3. Originality and attribution audit}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Source-text overlap (internal check, not a similarity service).} The paper body was compared with the full or abstract text of 13 cited sources that could be downloaded: five arXiv abstracts, RFC 7593, 8826 and 8828, the mDNS Internet-Draft, and the Sansec, AirSnitch, Framing Frames and MacStealer pages. Only 18 of 8{,}044 words (0.22\%) fall in any run of six identical words, and each run is a title or fixed term (e.g.\ ``client isolation in Wi-Fi networks''). No passage needed rewriting for overlap.
\item \textbf{Not covered:} the full text of paywalled papers (Nibert, Cox, Framing Frames full paper, AirSnitch full paper), the wider web, and student-paper databases. Only Turnitin or iThenticate can cover those.
\item \textbf{Attribution.} Every leakage class, attack step, and countermeasure in Tables II, IV and V names its source or is marked as an assumption (A1--A4). The literature-search description (Google Scholar, seven queries, 41 sources) matches the authors' own Research Gap Analysis document (August 2026).
\item \textbf{Repetition.} No filler transitions (``furthermore'', ``moreover'', ``it is important to note'') occur. The only phrase repeated four or more times is ``Wi-Fi client isolation'', the subject of the paper.
\item \textbf{AI-detector score: not established.} No AI-detection service is available in this environment, so no score can be reported. Detector scores are unreliable on technical writing. The paper discloses AI assistance as IEEE requires, which is what protects the authors.
\item \textbf{Similarity percentage: not established.} No similarity-checking service was run. The 0.22\% figure above is an internal comparison against 13 sources only and must not be presented as a similarity report.
\end{itemize}

\section*{5. Scientific and reproducibility audit}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Emulation (Tables VI, VII).} Reproduced from raw data as stated in Section 1. Re-running the sessions themselves needs root in WSL/Linux, coturn 4.5.2, and the Playwright browser builds named in the paper; all scripts are in \texttt{experiment/}. Not re-run today.
\item \textbf{Laptop check (Sec.\ VIII-D).} 60 sessions, 0 errors; counts and medians match \texttt{real\_host\_results.jsonl}. Re-runnable with \texttt{real\_host\_probe.py}.
\item \textbf{iPhone check (Sec.\ VIII-E).} Evidence is two screenshots; the server's \texttt{results.jsonl} was saved on another laptop and is not in the repository. The paper reports exactly what the screenshots show and states $n=2$.
\item \textbf{Statistics.} Exact (Clopper--Pearson) 95\% lower bounds for 8/8, 16/16 and 56/56 recomputed: 0.631, 0.794, 0.936. Correct.
\item \textbf{Known unexplained result.} Raw-host relay failures under NAT with isolation and 20~ms delay are reported as unexplained. This is honest, but a reviewer will ask about it.
\item \textbf{Abstract and conclusion} agree with the results; no claim exceeds the data.
\end{itemize}
